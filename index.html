Jun 2022 – Present • Austin, Texas

  • Architected CI/CD on GitHub Actions + Fastlane: a single required PR gate, Slack reporting, self-healing Unity licensing, and iOS canary builds for a Bazel migration.
  • Merged 200+ pull requests in the past year; maintain 20+ public Unity ad-network adapters and cloud-updatable (AWS S3) adapter tooling.
  • While Chartboost was a Take-Two company (through early 2025), partnered directly with Zynga and 2K Games teams to integrate and debug our technologies.
